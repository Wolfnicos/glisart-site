Delete my data

Français

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Glisart › Settings tab.
  2. “Delete my photos and looks”: erases the photos and looks saved on your phone.
  3. “Delete my account”: deletes your account on our servers. Your sessions and your device key are revoked immediately, the link with Apple or Google is erased (and access is revoked with Apple), and your usage history is made anonymous.
  4. “Withdraw my agreement”: no photo is sent any more until you agree again.

Without the app

Write to support@glisart.com from your address, with “Account deletion” and the approximate date of your last try-on. We handle the request within 30 days and confirm the deletion.

What is deleted, what remains

DataAfter deletion
Photos and looks on the phoneErased
Account, device, sessionsRevoked immediately
Usage historyMade anonymous; only a non-personal technical counter remains, to prevent getting the free offer several times
Encrypted backupsDisappear automatically within 30 days
PhotosNever kept on our servers
PurchasesReceipts stay with Apple or Google, under their legal obligations

Unused credits are lost when the account is deleted.
