1. The service

Glisart is an AI simulation app:

  • Manicure try-on: from a photo of your hand and your description, Glisart suggests variations and shows the result on your hand, with the product list, the steps and a card for your nail salon.
  • Match my outfit: from a photo of your outfit, Glisart suggests matching manicures.
  • Dress me and My nails, my outfit: from a full-length photo of you (or of your nails), Glisart suggests outfits and can show you wearing the chosen one.

Results are indicative: colour, shape, fit and the real result may differ. Glisart is neither a salon nor a shop and provides no physical service.

2. Age and account

  • Glisart is intended for people aged 16 and over. “Dress me” is for adults only.
  • You can use Glisart without an account (a guest account is created automatically on your phone). An Apple or Google account is needed to buy credits and to get them back on another phone.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings › “Delete my account”.

3. Photos and rules of use

  • Only photograph yourself (your hand, nails, outfit, full-length photo), or an adult who agrees.
  • You must not use Glisart to depict a person without their consent, to produce sexual or intimate images, or illegal, misleading or hateful content or content infringing the rights of others, or to bypass the technical limits of the service. For “Dress me”, the photo is checked (one person, adult in appearance) before any image.
  • Every result carries the label “AI-modified image”; do not remove it to pass a result off as a real photo.

4. Credits

  • What costs credits: one image (try-on on your hand or Dress me) = 10 credits; 3 suggested outfits = 2 credits. Outfit analysis and manicure suggestions are free. The cost is shown in the app before each action.
  • Given once: 1 outfit idea without an account; with an Apple or Google account, 3 ideas in total and 1 image (the free image is reserved for the first accounts, while the offer lasts).
  • Packs: 50, 150 or 400 credits, bought in the app through Apple’s payment system (App Store) or Google’s (Google Play). The price of each pack is shown in the app, taxes included, before purchase. There is no subscription.
  • Credits are added to your account as soon as Apple or Google confirms the purchase. They never expire, are linked to your account, personal, non-transferable and not refundable in cash.
  • An image that fails (photo refused, technical error, result not delivered) uses no credits: credits are given back automatically.
  • Unused credits are lost when the account is deleted.

5. Refunds

In-app purchases are billed by Apple (or Google). Refunds follow Apple’s policy (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play’s. Credits from a refunded purchase are removed from the account.

6. Your content

You remain the owner of your photos. You only allow us to process them for the time needed to perform the requested feature (see the privacy policy). You may freely use the results for personal purposes. Your saved looks stay on your phone.

8. Reporting

A shocking or inappropriate result can be reported in the app (“Report this result”) or at support@glisart.com; we review it promptly.
