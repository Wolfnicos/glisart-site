Help & contact

Français

Write to us: support@glisart.com
We answer in French, English and Romanian, usually within 48 business hours.

My photo is refused

Place one hand flat, nails visible, in soft light, without gloves or rings hiding the nails. The app checks the photo before anything is sent and tells you what is wrong.

The result does not match my request

After your wish, Glisart shows what it understood and suggests three variations. You can change the text or adjust the shade, finish and shape before the try-on. A try-on that fails uses no credits.

I changed phone

Your credits are linked to your Apple or Google account. On the new phone, open Glisart › Settings › Account and sign in with the same Apple or Google account: your credits come back. Your saved looks stay on the old phone (they are stored only on the device). A problem? Write to us with the purchase date.

How much does an image cost?

One image (try-on on your hand or Dress me) costs 10 credits; 3 suggested outfits cost 2 credits; outfit analysis and manicure suggestions are free. Credits come in packs (50, 150 or 400), with no subscription, and never expire. Details in the terms.

Refund of a purchase

Payments go through Apple or Google: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or in Google Play.

Report an inappropriate result

Use “Report this result” under the result, or send us the date and time of the try-on at support@glisart.com. We do not keep your photos: attach the result if you want us to review it.
