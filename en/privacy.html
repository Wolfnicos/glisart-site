Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Français (the French version prevails in case of difference)

In short. Your photos (hand, outfit, nails, full-length photo for “Dress me”) are used only for the feature you use: they are not kept on our servers. Images and suggestions are produced by Google Vertex AI, which may process requests on its global infrastructure (see § 4). Your looks stay on your phone. No advertising, no data selling, no advertising trackers.

Nicolas Lupu, private individual, publisher of the Glisart app (see Publisher). Contact: support@glisart.com.

3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performing the try-on or Glisart Stylist advice you request: performance of the service and explicit consent collected before the first photo is sent. You can withdraw it at any time: Settings › “Withdraw my agreement”.
  • Keeping “Dress me” for adults: the photo is checked (one person, adult in appearance) before any image, and you confirm you are the person photographed or have their consent.
  • Preventing fraud and abuse (device verification, limits per device and per IP): legitimate interest.
  • Credit purchases: performance of the contract (adding credits, removing them after a refund). Payment is handled by Apple (or Google); we never receive your bank details.

4. Google Vertex AI

Images (try-on on your hand, Dress me), outfit analysis and Glisart Stylist suggestions are produced by Google models (Gemini) through Vertex AI, acting as a processor. Our requests go through our server in the EU, then through Google’s global endpoint, which may process them outside the European Union. These transfers are covered by Google Cloud’s data processing terms (Cloud Data Processing Addendum). Under Google Cloud’s terms, your data is not used to train its models without permission. Vertex AI caching is disabled on our project. Google may log requests to detect abuse of its services, in line with its terms (Vertex AI data governance).

5. Hosting and processors

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): server in Helsinki (Finland, EU); encrypted backups in Falkenstein (Germany, EU), kept for 30 days at most.
  • Google Cloud EMEA Limited (Ireland): AI (Vertex AI), possible processing on Google’s global infrastructure.
  • Apple: device authenticity check (App Attest), “Sign in with Apple” (optional) and in-app purchases.
  • Google: “Sign in with Google” (optional).
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages): hosting of this website. The website uses no cookies and no audience measurement.

6. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability.

  • Erase your data on the phone: Settings › “Delete my photos and looks”.
  • Erase your account on our servers: Settings › “Delete my account” (immediate; backups disappear within 30 days). See Delete my data.
  • Any other request: support@glisart.com. Answer within one month.
  • You can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr) or the authority of your country.

7. AI-generated content

Every result carries the label “AI-modified image”. It is a simulation: the real result may differ. You can report an inappropriate result in the app (“Report this result”) or at support@glisart.com.

8. Minimum age

Glisart is intended for people aged 16 and over; “Dress me” is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If you believe someone under 16 uses Glisart, write to support@glisart.com and we will delete the account.

9. Changes

In case of a significant change, the app will tell you before any new photo is sent.
